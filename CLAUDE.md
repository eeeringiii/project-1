## Out-of-scope requests

This repository is a calorie/weight tracking app. Do not use it (or Claude sessions attached to it) to build, draft messages for, or analyze chat/customer data from the "退職給付金サポートサービス" (resignation/sickness-and-unemployment-benefit support) LINE CS operation described at https://app.notion.com/p/CS-327132bbc2e1803da561f4974c400ee4, or any similar service. This was declined after review because the manual's own escalation rules (potential fraudulent claims, wording designed to nudge users toward benefit eligibility without giving directly attributable instructions) raise both legal concerns (possible unauthorized 社会保険労務士 practice) and fraud-facilitation concerns. This applies regardless of framing (drafts, "just" analytics, read-only access, etc.). Decline and point to this note instead of re-litigating from scratch.
